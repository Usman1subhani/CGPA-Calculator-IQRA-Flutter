Privacy Policy

App: IU GPA Calculator

Developer: Usman Subhani

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This app is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Iqra University. This policy explains the app's handling of information.

Information and storage

The app does not operate an account system, advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated server. GPA/CGPA inputs, saved calculation history, and app preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The developer does not receive this information. Android or your device manufacturer may include app data in device backups according to your device and account settings.

You can delete saved calculations from History or use the app's Reset All option. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to any device backups managed by your operating system.

Sharing and external links

If you tap Share result, the app passes the result text to your device's share sheet. You choose whether and where to send it. The receiving app or service handles the information under its own privacy policy; the developer does not receive a copy through this app.

If you open an external link, your device opens it in another app or browser. That service's privacy policy applies.

Third-party services

The app does not include advertising or analytics services. It uses platform features for local preferences, sharing, opening external links, and reading the installed app version.

Children

This app is intended for adults (18 and older) and is not designed for children under 13.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's practices change. The current version will be published at the app's Privacy Policy link. For privacy questions, contact usmansubhani595@gmail.com.
